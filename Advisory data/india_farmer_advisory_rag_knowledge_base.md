### [CHUNK-02] Indian Agro-Climatic Zones & Cropping Seasons Calendar
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: agro_climatic_zones` | `seasons: kharif_rabi_zaid` | `region: pan_india_and_eastern_india`
* **Seasonal Classification in Indian Agriculture:**
  * **Kharif (Monsoon Season):** Sown with the onset of the Southwest Monsoon (June to July) and harvested in September to November ([ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories 2025](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/ICAR-En-Kharif-Agro-Advisories-for-Farmers-2025.pdf)). Major crops include *Aman* / Kharif Paddy (*Oryza sativa*), Maize, Bajra (Pearl Millet), Jowar (Sorghum), Pigeonpea (*Arhar/Tur*), Greengram (*Moong*), Blackgram (*Urad*), Groundnut, Soybean, Sesame, Cotton, Jute, and Sugarcane.
  * **Rabi (Winter Season):** Sown from October to December and harvested from March to May ([ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/Rabi-Agro-Advisory-2021-22_0.pdf)). Major crops include Wheat, *Boro* Paddy, Barley, Chickpea (*Gram/Chana*), Lentil (*Masoor*), Field Pea, Rapeseed-Mustard (*Toria/Sarson*), Potato, Linseed, Garlic, Onion, and winter cole vegetables (Cabbage, Cauliflower).
  * **Zaid / Pre-Kharif (Summer Season):** Sown between March and May between Rabi harvest and Kharif onset ([NFSM Package of Practices](https://nfsm.gov.in/ReadyReckoner/NFSM_Package.pdf)). Major crops include Summer *Moong*, Summer *Urad*, *Aus* Paddy, Jute (sown March–April in West Bengal), Forage Maize/Sorghum, Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Bitter Gourd, Ridge Gourd, Sponge Gourd, Pointed Gourd (*Parwal*), and Okra (*Bhindi*).

## 2. Soil Health Card (SHC) 12-Parameter Diagnostics & Fertilizer Conversion Engine

### [CHUNK-03] Soil Health Card (SHC) 12-Parameter Thresholds & Interpretation Matrix
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: soil_science` | `scheme: soil_health_card` | `parameters: pH_EC_OC_NPK_S_Zn_Fe_Cu_Mn_B`
* **Soil Sampling Standard Operating Procedure (SOP):** Collect composite soil samples after crop harvest and before applying manures or chemical fertilizers ([Soil Sampling & Testing Manual](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/Manual/140723_ManualClass11_12.pdf)). Sample in a zig-zag grid (15–20 sub-samples per uniform field up to 1–2 ha) at 0–15 cm plough depth for shallow-rooted field crops (rice, wheat, pulses, vegetables) and 0–30 cm for deep-rooted crops (cotton, sugarcane, pigeonpea). Always use a stainless-steel auger rather than a rusted iron *khurpi* or spade when testing for micronutrients (Fe, Zn, Cu, Mn) to avoid metallic contamination ([Soil Health Assessment Manual](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/Manual/140723DraftTeacherManual_PDF.pdf)). Avoid bunds, marshy patches, manure pits, recently fertilized bands, and shade areas within 5 meters of trees.
* **Soil Health Card Validity & Renewal:** Cards are issued at 3-year intervals across 12 chemical and physical parameters and can be generated via the [Soil Health Card Portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/) or rapid digital kits such as the [PUSA STFR Meter Kit](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/Manual/140723_ManualClass11_12.pdf).

### [CHUNK-04] Nutrient-to-Commercial Fertilizer Conversion Formulas & Biofertilizer Rules
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: fertilizer_calculation` | `calculation: npk_to_urea_dap_ssp_mop` | `biofertilizers: icar_guidelines`
* **Exact Mathematical Conversion Multipliers for LLM Advisory Generation:** When a farmer query asks *"How many kg or bags of Urea, DAP/SSP, and MOP should I apply per hectare or per acre/bigha?"* based on a Recommended Dose of Fertilizer ($\text{RDF}$ in $\text{kg } \text{N} : \text{P}_2\text{O}_5 : \text{K}_2\text{O}\text{ per ha}$), the RAG system must compute commercial fertilizer quantities using these standard nutrient compositions ([Fertilizer Recommendation Portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/fertilizer-dosage); [Best Fertilizers Guide](https://fertilizerstore.in/blog/best-fertilizers-for-crop)):
  1. **Combination A (Using Neem-Coated Urea + Single Super Phosphate [SSP] + Muriate of Potash [MOP]):**
     * **Neem-Coated Urea ($46\%\text{ N}$):** $\text{Urea (kg)} = \text{Required N (kg)} \times 2.174$
     * **Single Super Phosphate – SSP ($16\%\text{ P}_2\text{O}_5, 11\%\text{ S}, 21\%\text{ Ca}$):** $\text{SSP (kg)} = \text{Required P}_2\text{O}_5\text{ (kg)} \times 6.25$
     * **Muriate of Potash – MOP ($60\%\text{ K}_2\text{O}$):** $\text{MOP (kg)} = \text{Required K}_2\text{O}\text{ (kg)} \times 1.667$
     * *Agronomic Rule:* Prefer Combination A (SSP-based) for oilseeds (mustard, groundnut, sesame, soybean) and pulses because SSP supplies critical Sulphur ($11\%\text{ S}$) alongside Phosphorus ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)).
  2. **Combination B (Using Di-Ammonium Phosphate [DAP] + Neem-Coated Urea + Muriate of Potash [MOP]):**
     * **Step 1 – Compute DAP ($18\%\text{ N}, 46\%\text{ P}_2\text{O}_5$):** $\text{DAP (kg)} = \text{Required P}_2\text{O}_5\text{ (kg)} \times 2.174$
     * **Step 2 – Deduct Nitrogen Supplied by DAP:** $\text{N from DAP (kg)} = \text{DAP (kg)} \times 0.18$
     * **Step 3 – Compute Remaining Urea ($46\%\text{ N}$):** $\text{Urea (kg)} = (\text{Required N (kg)} - \text{N from DAP (kg)}) \times 2.174$
     * **Step 4 – Compute MOP ($60\%\text{ K}_2\text{O}$):** $\text{MOP (kg)} = \text{Required K}_2\text{O}\text{ (kg)} \times 1.667$
  3. **Combination C (Using NPK Complex $10:26:26$ or $12:32:16$ as Basal):**
     * **NPK $12:32:16$:** Every $100\text{ kg}$ supplies $12\text{ kg N}$, $32\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5$, and $16\text{ kg K}_2\text{O}$ ([Rabi Fertilizer Guide](https://fertilizerstore.in/blog/best-fertilizers-for-crop)).
     * **Unit Conversions for Indian Farmers:** $1\text{ Hectare (ha)} = 2.471\text{ Acres} = 7.47\text{ Standard Bengal Bighas}$ ($1\text{ Acre} = 3\text{ Standard Bengal Bighas}$ of $1,333.33\text{ m}^2$ each). To convert $\text{kg/ha}$ to $\text{kg/acre}$, multiply by $0.4047$; to convert $\text{kg/ha}$ to $\text{kg/Bengal Bigha}$, divide by $7.5$.
* **Biofertilizer & Liquid Consortia Integration Rules ([ICAR Biofertilizer Technologies](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-06/ICAR-technologies-Biofertilizers.pdf)):**
  * **Liquid Biofertilizers (e.g., *Bio Phos*, *Azotobacter*, *Azospirillum*, *Rhizobium*, KSB):** Save up to $25–30\text{ kg P/ha}$ and $20–25\text{ kg N/ha}$ while boosting grain yield of rice, wheat, maize, and mustard by $7–11\%$ ([ICAR Biofertilizers](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-06/ICAR-technologies-Biofertilizers.pdf)).
  * **Compatibility Sequence Rule (Seed Treatment):** Always follow the strict **"FIR" Sequence (Fungicide $\rightarrow$ Insecticide $\rightarrow$ Rhizobium/Biofertilizer)**. Treat seeds with chemical fungicide/insecticide first, dry in shade for 2–4 hours, and apply biofertilizer (*Azotobacter*, PSB, *Rhizobium*, *Trichoderma viride*) last just before sowing; never mix chemical pesticides directly with live microbial inoculants ([ICAR Okra ICM Guide](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IndHort/article/download/115798/47512/472864)).

## 3. Comprehensive Crop Production Advisories (Cereals, Millets, Pulses, Oilseeds & Cash Crops)

### [CHUNK-05] Rice / Paddy (*Oryza sativa*) — Kharif (*Aman*), Summer (*Boro*), Pre-Kharif (*Aus*) & SRI
* **Metadata Tags:** `crop: rice_paddy` | `seasons: aman_boro_aus` | `practices: transplanted_sri_dsr` | `region: pan_india_west_bengal`
* **Varietal & Seasonal Classification:**
  * **Kharif / *Aman* Paddy (Sown June–July, Harvested Nov–Dec):** Short-duration (115–125 days: *IR-36*, *Shatabdi/IET-4786*, *MTU-1010*, *Sahbhagi Dhan* for drought tolerance), Medium-duration (130–140 days: *MTU-7029 / Swarna*, *Swarna Sub-1* for 14-day submergence tolerance, *Pratikshya*), and Long-duration/Aromatic (*Gobindobhog*, *Pusa Basmati 1121*, *Pusa Basmati 1509*).
  * **Rabi / Summer *Boro* Paddy (Nursery Nov–Dec, Transplanted Jan–Feb, Harvested April–May):** High-yielding irrigated crop in West Bengal, Assam, Bihar, and Odisha ([RKVY West Bengal SAP](https://rkvy.da.gov.in/static/SAP/WB/XII%20Plan/SAP%20XII%20Plan-West%20Bengal.pdf)). Popular cultivars: *Shatabdi (IET-4786)*, *Khitish (IET-4094)*, *IR-64*, *MTU-1010*, *Naveen*.
* **Seed Rate, Nursery Management & Stand Establishment ([TNAU Rice Stand Establishment](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropproduction_cereals_rice_tranpudlow_mainfield_stand_establish.html)):**
  * **Conventional Wet-Bed Transplanting:** Seed rate is $30–40\text{ kg/ha}$ for fine/short-grain varieties, $40–50\text{ kg/ha}$ for medium varieties, and $55–60\text{ kg/ha}$ for coarse varieties (in West Bengal *Aman* seedbeds: $15–16\text{ kg/acre}$ fine, $16–20\text{ kg/acre}$ medium, $22–24\text{ kg/acre}$ coarse per [Aman Paddy Protocol](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363291414_Mixed_Cultivation_of_Different_Vegetables_along_with_Aman_Paddy_in_West_Bengal)). For F1 Hybrids, use $15\text{ kg/ha}$ fresh seed every season — farmers must never reuse harvested grain from F1 hybrid rice as seed because segregation causes severe yield loss ([TNAU Crop Production FAQs](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_faqs_cropproduction.html)).
  * **Seedling Age Rule:** Transplant seedlings at the 4-leaf stage ($18–22\text{ days}$ for short-duration varieties, $25–30\text{ days}$ for medium/long-duration). Transplanting aged seedlings (5th leaf stage or older) reduces tillering; if delayed due to late monsoon, increase seedlings from $2–3\text{ per hill}$ to $4–5\text{ per hill}$ with closer spacing ($15 \times 10\text{ cm}$ or $20 \times 10\text{ cm}$) and increase basal nitrogen ([TNAU Rice Guide](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropproduction_cereals_rice_tranpudlow_mainfield_stand_establish.html)).
  * **System of Rice Intensification (SRI):** Achieves mean grain yields around $5.94\text{ t/ha}$ with $30–40\%$ water savings ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)). Key pillars: (1) $5–7.5\text{ kg seed/ha}$ on raised mat nursery, (2) Transplant single young seedling ($8–12\text{ days old}$, 2-leaf stage) within 30 minutes of uprooting at shallow depth ($1–2\text{ cm}$), (3) Wide square grid spacing of $25 \times 25\text{ cm}$ ($16\text{ hills/m}^2$), (4) Cono-weeder operation at $10, 20, 30,\text{ and }40\text{ DAT}$ to aerate roots and incorporate weeds, and (5) Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) irrigation ($2.5\text{ cm}$ water applied only after hairline soil cracks appear up to panicle initiation).
* **Fertilizer & Nutrient Schedule ($\text{N} : \text{P}_2\text{O}_5 : \text{K}_2\text{O}$):**
  * **Kharif (*Aman*) HYV:** $80:40:40\text{ kg/ha}$ (for short-duration) to $120:60:60\text{ kg/ha}$ (for high-yielding medium/long-duration).
  * **Summer (*Boro*) Irrigated HYV:** $120–150\text{ kg N} : 60–75\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 60–75\text{ kg K}_2\text{O per ha}$ plus $\text{ZnSO}_4 \cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O} \text{ @ } 25\text{ kg/ha}$ basal ([ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories 2025](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/ICAR-En-Kharif-Agro-Advisories-for-Farmers-2025.pdf)).
  * **Split Application Timing:** Apply $25\%\text{ N} + 100\%\text{ P}_2\text{O}_5 + 50\%\text{ K}_2\text{O}$ as basal at final puddling; $50\%\text{ N}$ at Active Tillering ($21\text{ DAT}$); and $25\%\text{ N} + 50\%\text{ K}_2\text{O}$ at Panicle Initiation ($42–45\text{ DAT}$). Drain standing water to a thin film ($<2\text{ cm}$) before top-dressing Urea and re-irrigating after $24–36\text{ hours}$.
* **Critical Irrigation & Harvesting Criteria:**
  * Critical moisture stages: Seedling establishment ($1–7\text{ DAT}$), Active Tillering, Panicle Initiation (booting), Flowering/Anthesis, and Milk/Soft Dough stage. Drain field completely $10–12\text{ days}$ before harvest when $80–85\%$ of panicle spikelets turn golden yellow.
  * Safe storage grain moisture: Dry harvested paddy to $14\%$ moisture for raw rice and $15\%$ for parboiled rice ([TNAU Crop Harvesting Criteria](http://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropharvesting.html)).

### [CHUNK-06] Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) & Maize (*Zea mays*) — Rabi & Kharif Advisories
* **Metadata Tags:** `crop: wheat_maize` | `seasons: rabi_kharif` | `practices: zero_tillage_cri_irrigation`
* **Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) Production Protocol ([NFSM Ready Reckoner](https://nfsm.gov.in/ReadyReckoner/NFSM_Package.pdf); [ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/Rabi-Agro-Advisory-2021-22_0.pdf)):**
  * **Soils & Sowing Window:** Thrives in well-drained loam, clay loam, sandy loam, and black soils with pH $6.0–7.5$ ([NFSM Package](https://nfsm.gov.in/ReadyReckoner/NFSM_Package.pdf)). Timely sowing window is **November 1 to November 25** (optimal temperature $20–25^\circ\text{C}$ at germination, $14–16^\circ\text{C}$ at tillering). For late-sown wheat (after rice/potato harvest in December up to December 25), use short-duration heat-tolerant cultivars (*DBW-187*, *DBW-173*, *HD-2967*, *HD-3059*, *HD-3226*) and zero-tillage Happy Seeder drilling directly into rice stubbles to save $10–15\text{ days}$ and avoid terminal heat stress in March.
  * **Seed Rate & Spacing:** $100\text{ kg/ha}$ for timely-sown medium-grain varieties ($20–22.5\text{ cm}$ row spacing at $4–5\text{ cm}$ depth); increase to $125\text{ kg/ha}$ ($15–18\text{ cm}$ row spacing) for late-sown or rainfed conditions ([NFSM Package](https://nfsm.gov.in/ReadyReckoner/NFSM_Package.pdf)). Treat seeds with Carboxin $37.5\% + \text{Thiram } 37.5\%\text{ DS @ } 2.5\text{ g/kg}$ or Tebuconazole $2\%\text{ DS @ } 1.5\text{ g/kg}$ against loose smut.
  * **Fertilizer Schedule ($\text{NPK}$):** $120–150\text{ kg N} : 60\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 40\text{ kg K}_2\text{O per ha}$ for timely-sown irrigated wheat ($90:60:40\text{ kg/ha}$ for late-sown; $60:30:20\text{ kg/ha}$ for rainfed). Apply $50\%\text{ N} + 100\%\text{ P} + 100\%\text{ K}$ at sowing, $25\%\text{ N}$ immediately after first irrigation (CRI stage), and $25\%\text{ N}$ at second irrigation (tillering/jointing).
  * **6 Critical Irrigation Stages in Wheat:**
    1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI) Stage ($20–25\text{ DAS}$):** *Most critical stage* — missing irrigation at CRI reduces grain yield by $25–30\%$. If only 1 irrigation is available, apply at CRI.
    2. **Late Tillering ($40–45\text{ DAS}$):** Apply here + CRI if 2 irrigations are available (CRI and Boot/Flowering).
    3. **Late Jointing ($60–65\text{ DAS}$).**
    4. **Flowering / Anthesis ($80–85\text{ DAS}$):** Prevents pollen sterility under rising temperatures.
    5. **Milk Stage ($100–105\text{ DAS}$):** Mitigates terminal heat stress (supplement with $2\%\text{ KNO}_3$ or $0.2\%\text{ MOP}$ foliar spray if March daytime temperature exceeds $32^\circ\text{C}$).
    6. **Dough Stage ($115–120\text{ DAS}$).** Harvest and dry grain to $12\%$ moisture content for safe storage ([TNAU Harvesting Criteria](http://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropharvesting.html)).
* **Maize (*Zea mays*) Production Protocol ([TNAU Rainfed Maize](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/millets_maize_rainfed_maize.html); [TNAU Fodder Maize](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/foragecrops_fodder_maize_crop_production.html)):**
  * **Land Preparation & Sub-Soil Chisel Ploughing:** For fields with shallow hard-pan formation, chisel-plough at $0.5\text{ m}$ intervals in two perpendicular directions once every 3 years and apply $12.5\text{ t/ha}$ FYM or composted coir pith to boost rainwater retention ([TNAU Rainfed Maize](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/millets_maize_rainfed_maize.html)). Sow on ridges and furrows ($60 \times 20\text{ cm}$ for grain hybrids @ $20\text{ kg seed/ha}$; $30 \times 15\text{ cm}$ for fodder maize @ $40–50\text{ kg/ha}$ with $30:40:20\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ per [TNAU Fodder Maize](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/foragecrops_fodder_maize_crop_production.html)).
  * **Grain Maize Fertilizer Schedule:** $150:75:75\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ for Single Cross Hybrids in Kharif and $180–200:80:80\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ for high-yielding Rabi maize ($25\%\text{ N}$ + full P and K basal, $50\%\text{ N}$ at knee-high stage $25–30\text{ DAS}$, $25\%\text{ N}$ at tasseling $45–50\text{ DAS}$) plus $25\text{ kg ZnSO}_4\text{/ha}$ to prevent "White Bud" zinc deficiency.
  * **Critical Water Sensitivity:** Maize is highly sensitive to both waterlogging (at seedling/knee-high stage — provide drainage channels) and moisture stress at **Tasseling and Silking**; dry grain to $12\%$ moisture before storage ([TNAU Harvesting Criteria](http://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropharvesting.html)).

### [CHUNK-07] Pulses, Oilseeds & Commercial Cash Crops (Mustard, Groundnut, Chickpea, Potato, Jute, Cotton, Sugarcane)
* **Metadata Tags:** `crop: pulses_oilseeds_potato_jute_cotton_sugarcane` | `seasons: kharif_rabi` | `region: pan_india_west_bengal`
* **Rapeseed-Mustard (*Brassica juncea* / *Brassica campestris*) ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf); [ResearchGate Cropping Pattern](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/283178674_Improvement_from_mustard-Boro-T_Aman_cropping_pattern_to_mustard-Boro-jute-T_Aman)):**
  * **Sowing & Cropping Sequence:** Sown October–November ($5–6\text{ kg seed/ha}$ at $30 \times 10\text{ cm}$ spacing, thinned at $15–20\text{ DAS}$). In Eastern India and Assam, *Toria* relay-sown at the dough stage of *Aman* rice or in a 4-crop sequence (*Mustard – Boro Rice – Jute/Aus – T. Aman Rice*) raises rice-equivalent yield to $20.82\text{ t/ha}$ ([Four-Crop Pattern Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360005282_Development_of_Four_Crop_Based_Cropping_Pattern_Mustard-Boro-T_Aus-T_Aman_Against_Fallow-Boro-_Fallow-T_Aman_Rice)).
  * **Nutrient & Sulphur Requirement:** Apply $80–100\text{ kg N} : 40\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 40\text{ kg K}_2\text{O per ha}$ irrigated ($40:20:20$ rainfed) plus **$20–40\text{ kg Sulphur/ha}$** (via SSP or Bentonite S) and $1\text{ kg Boron/ha}$ (Borax @ $10\text{ kg/ha}$) to maximize oil glucosinolate synthesis and siliqua filling ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)). Critical irrigations: Pre-flowering ($30–35\text{ DAS}$) and Siliqua/Pod development ($60–65\text{ DAS}$).
* **Groundnut (*Arachis hypogaea*) ([TNAU Groundnut Guide](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/CropProduction/Oilseeds/oilseeds_groundnut01.html); [ICAR-IIGR Crop Production](https://icar-iigr.org.in/crop-production/)):**
  * **Seed Rate, Spacing & Gypsum Application:** Bunch types require $120–125\text{ kg kernels/ha}$ ($30 \times 10\text{ cm}$ spacing). Apply $20:40:60\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ basal ($25:50:75\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ irrigated). **Mandatory Gypsum Application:** Apply $400\text{ kg/ha}$ of finely powdered Gypsum by side-placement and earthing-up at $40–45\text{ DAS}$ (pegging stage); Calcium in the pegging zone ($0–8\text{ cm}$) is absorbed directly by developing pods to prevent "pops" (empty pods), while Sulphur boosts oil percentage.
  * **Intercropping Nutrient Rule ([ICAR-IIGR](https://icar-iigr.org.in/crop-production/)):** In Groundnut + Maize (at Jhargram, West Bengal) or Groundnut + Sorghum/Ragi intercropping, apply $100\%\text{ RDN}$ as basal to Groundnut and $50\%\text{ RDN}$ to the intercropped cereal; in Groundnut + Bt-Cotton ($3:1$ row ratio), apply $75\%\text{ RDF}$ to Groundnut and $100\%\text{ RDF}$ to Bt-Cotton.
* **Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*) & Kharif/Summer Pulses (*Arhar, Moong, Urad*) ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)):**
  * **Starter Dose & Foliar Spray:** Legumes fix atmospheric nitrogen via root nodules when treated with crop-specific *Rhizobium* ($200\text{ g/10 kg seed}$) + PSB ($200\text{ g/10 kg seed}$) + Ammonium Molybdate ($1\text{ g/kg seed}$). Apply starter dose of $20\text{ kg N} : 40–50\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 20\text{ kg K}_2\text{O} : 20\text{ kg S per ha}$ basal.
  * **Nipping & Urea/DAP Foliar Spray in Chickpea:** Practice apical bud nipping at $35–40\text{ DAS}$ (before flowering) to promote lateral branching. In rainfed chickpea, foliar spray of $2\%\text{ Urea}$ or $2\%\text{ DAP}$ ($20\text{ g/liter water}$) at flower initiation and 10 days later increases grain yield by $\sim 20\%$ over untreated crops ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)). Never irrigate chickpea during peak flowering (causes flower drop); irrigate at pre-flowering (branching) and pod-filling stages.
* **Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*) ([Vikaspedia Potato Package](https://agriculture.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/agriculture/crop-production/package-of-practices/vegetables-1/potato?lgn=en); [ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop-Management_1.pdf)):**
  * **Planting & Seed Tuber Norms:** Major Rabi cash crop in West Bengal (Hooghly, Burdwan, Bankura, Howrah, Terai), UP, Bihar, and Punjab ([UBKV Research](https://www.ubkv.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/RRSTZ-2017-2018.pdf)). Plant between late October and mid-November when maximum temperature is $28–30^\circ\text{C}$ and minimum is $18–20^\circ\text{C}$ (tuberization requires $18–20^\circ\text{C}$ night temperature). Use disease-free seed tubers ($30–40\text{ g}$ size with 2–3 eyes, $2.5–3.0\text{ t/ha}$ seed rate) spaced at $60 \times 20\text{ cm}$. Treat cut/whole tubers in Mancozeb $75\%\text{ WP @ } 2.5\text{ g/liter}$ + $3\%\text{ Boric Acid}$ for 20 minutes and shade-dry for 24 hours.
  * **Nutrient & Earthing-Up Schedule:** Apply $20\text{ t/ha FYM}$ + $150–200\text{ kg N} : 100–150\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 100–150\text{ kg K}_2\text{O per ha}$ ($50\%\text{ N}$ + full P and K basal in furrows; $50\%\text{ N}$ at first earthing-up at $25–30\text{ DAP}$ when plants are $15–20\text{ cm}$ tall to prevent greening/solanine accumulation in exposed tubers) ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop-Management_1.pdf)). Potato + Garlic ($1:1$ intercropping) with $100\%\text{ RDF}$ is highly profitable ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop-Management_1.pdf)). Stop irrigation $10–12\text{ days}$ before haulm cutting (*dehaulming*) for skin curing.
* **Jute (*Corchorus olitorius* – Tossa Jute & *Corchorus capsularis* – White Jute) ([RKVY West Bengal SAP](https://rkvy.da.gov.in/static/SAP/WB/XII%20Plan/SAP%20XII%20Plan-West%20Bengal.pdf)):**
  * **Sowing & Nutrient Schedule:** Sown mid-March to April in West Bengal (*Naveen/JRO-524*, *Suren/JRO-204*). Seed rate: $5–6\text{ kg/ha}$ (Tossa line sowing at $25 \times 5–7\text{ cm}$) or $7–8\text{ kg/ha}$ (White jute). Apply $60–80\text{ kg N} : 30–40\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5 : 30–40\text{ kg K}_2\text{O per ha}$ ($1/3\text{ N}$ + full P and K basal; $1/3\text{ N}$ at $21\text{ DAS}$ after first weeding/thinning; $1/3\text{ N}$ at $35–42\text{ DAS}$).
  * **Harvesting & Microbial Retting (CRIJAF SONA):** Harvest at 110–120 days (small pod stage) for optimal fiber strength and luster. Use slow-moving clean water ($1:20$ plant-to-water ratio), submerge bundles with concrete blocks or water hyacinth (never mud or banana stems, which stain fiber black via iron-tannin reaction), and apply *CRIJAF SONA* microbial formulation @ $25–30\text{ kg/ha}$ to shorten retting by $6–8\text{ days}$ and improve fiber grade by 1–2 grades.
* **Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*) & Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*) ([NFSM Commercial Crops](https://nfsm.gov.in/ReadyReckoner/CU4/CU4_Package2018.pdf); [ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/Rabi-Agro-Advisory-2021-22_0.pdf)):**
  * **Bt-Cotton:** Sow in May–June at $90 \times 60\text{ cm}$ or $120 \times 45\text{ cm}$ with refugia border crop (maize/bajra/sorghum) and cowpea intercrop ([NRIIPM Cotton IPM](https://nriipm.res.in/ipmmodules.aspx)). Apply $150:75:75\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ ($50\%$ soil + $50\%$ drip fertigation + $10\text{ kg ZnSO}_4\text{/ha}$ per [ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)) + two foliar sprays of $2\%\text{ KNO}_3$ + $1\%\text{ MgSO}_4$ at peak flowering and boll development to prevent leaf reddening (*Lalya*). Terminate crop by end of December to break the Pink Bollworm carry-over cycle ([NRIIPM Modules](https://nriipm.res.in/ipmmodules.aspx)).
  * **Sugarcane:** Recommended fertilizer dose is **$250:115:115\text{ kg NPK/ha}$** for seasonal (*Suru*) planting and **$300:170:170\text{ kg NPK/ha}$** for *Adsali* / long-duration planting, with Nitrogen applied in 4 splits (planting, 6 weeks, 12 weeks, and final earthing-up at 16–18 weeks) ([ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/Rabi-Agro-Advisory-2021-22_0.pdf)).

### [CHUNK-09] Protected Cultivation (Polyhouse, Shade Net, Gerbera, Capsicum & Drip Fertigation)
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: protected_cultivation` | `structures: naturally_ventilated_polyhouse_shade_net` | `crops: gerbera_capsicum_cucumber_tomato`
* **Structural & Microclimate Norms ([Protected Cultivation Guide](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397744598_Protected_Cultivation_of_Vegetables_and_Flowers_Greenhouses_and_Polyhouses); [TNAU Gerbera Portal](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_flower%20crops_gerbera.html)):**
  * **Naturally Ventilated Polyhouse (NVPH):** Clad with $200\text{-micron}$ UV-stabilized 5-layer co-extruded polyethylene film ($80–85\%$ light transmission, UV-blocking & anti-drip inner coating) equipped with $40\text{-mesh}$ side-curtain insect nets and overhead $50\%$ aluminet/white shade net operated when solar radiation exceeds $60,000\text{ lux}$.
  * **Bed Sterilization & Preparation:** Before planting high-value crops (Gerbera, Colored Capsicum, Parthenocarpic Cucumber, Cherry Tomato), sterilize raised beds ($30–40\text{ cm}$ height, $90–100\text{ cm}$ top width, $45–50\text{ cm}$ walkway) via Soil Solarization (transparent $50\text{-micron}$ polythene for $4–6\text{ weeks}$ in summer) or Hydrogen Peroxide $50\% + \text{Silver } (\text{H}_2\text{O}_2 + \text{Ag}) \text{ @ } 35\text{ ml/m}^2$ in $1\text{ liter water/m}^2$, followed 7 days later by basal application of FYM ($10–15\text{ kg/m}^2$), Cocopeat ($2–3\text{ kg/m}^2$), Neem Cake ($200\text{ g/m}^2$), SSP ($50\text{ g/m}^2$), and *Trichoderma harzianum* + *Pseudomonas fluorescens* + *Paecilomyces lilacinus* ($10\text{ g each/m}^2$).
* **Gerbera (*Gerbera jamesonii*) Polyhouse Production Protocol ([TNAU Gerbera Guide](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_flower%20crops_gerbera.html); [Agrifirst Gerbera Polyhouse Norms](https://agrifirst.in/blog/economics-of-gerbera-polyhouse-farming); [ICAR-CCARI Gerbera DSS](https://ccari.res.in/dss/Gerbera.html)):**
  * **Target Environmental Setpoints:**
    * **Day Temperature:** $22^\circ\text{C} – 25^\circ\text{C}$ (operational envelope $18^\circ\text{C} – 35^\circ\text{C}$; above $32^\circ\text{C}$ activates overhead foggers and $50\%$ shade net).
    * **Night Temperature:** $12^\circ\text{C} – 16^\circ\text{C}$ ([TNAU Gerbera](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_flower%20crops_gerbera.html)).
    * **Relative Humidity (RH):** $60\% – 70\%$ ($75–80\%$ during initial 3 weeks post-planting for crown establishment; $>85\%$ RH triggers *Botrytis* gray mold and powdery mildew alerts).
    * **Light Intensity:** $43,000 – 65,000\text{ lux}$ ($400–450\text{ W/m}^2$ PAR) ([Agrifirst Gerbera Polyhouse](https://agrifirst.in/blog/economics-of-gerbera-polyhouse-farming)).
    * **Substrate pH & EC:** Root medium pH $5.5 – 6.5$ and EC $< 1.0 – 1.5\text{ dS/m}$.
  * **Planting Geometry & Crown Rule:** Plant tissue-cultured 4–5 leaf plug plants in 2 rows per bed at $30 \times 30\text{ cm}$ or $40 \times 30\text{ cm}$ ($\sim 6–7\text{ plants/m}^2$ or $24,000–26,000\text{ plants/acre}$). **Critical Crown Depth Rule:** Keep the crown of the Gerbera plant $1–2\text{ cm}$ *above* the soil surface; burying the crown causes fatal *Phytophthora cryptogea* crown rot, while planting too high desiccates roots ([TNAU Gerbera](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_flower%20crops_gerbera.html)). Disbud (remove initial flower buds) for the first $8–9\text{ weeks}$ to build strong vegetative leaf canopy.
  * **Gerbera & Polyhouse Vegetable Drip Fertigation Schedule ([ICAR Drip Fertigation Guide](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IndFarm/article/download/163803/62903/493520); [ICAR-CCARI Gerbera](https://ccari.res.in/dss/Gerbera.html)):**
    * **Vegetative Establishment (Weeks 1–12 after planting):** Apply Water-Soluble Fertilizer (WSF) $19:19:19$ or $20:20:20$ ($\text{N}:\text{P}_2\text{O}_5:\text{K}_2\text{O}$ in $1:1:1$ ratio) @ $0.4\text{ g/plant/day}$ (or $4\text{ g/plant}$ over splits per [ICAR-CCARI Gerbera](https://ccari.res.in/dss/Gerbera.html)) on alternate days via inline drip emitters ($2\text{ LPH}$ at $30\text{ cm}$ spacing, $4.5–7.0\text{ liters/m}^2\text{/day}$), maintaining irrigation water EC at $1.2–1.5\text{ dS/m}$ and pH $5.8–6.2$.
    * **Flowering & Reproductive Stage (From Week 13 onward for 2.5–3 years in Gerbera; Fruit-bulking in Capsicum/Cucumber):** Shift to high-Potassium ratio ($15:8:35$ or combination of Calcium Nitrate $\text{Ca(NO}_3)_2$, Mono-Potassium Phosphate $00:52:34$, and Potassium Nitrate $13:00:45$ + Sulphate of Potash $00:00:50$) to strengthen peduncle stalk rigidity, flower diameter ($10–12\text{ cm}$), and vase life ($10–14\text{ days}$). Never mix Calcium Nitrate in the same stock tank with sulphate or phosphate fertilizers (causes insoluble $\text{CaSO}_4$ / $\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2$ emitter clogging); always use a Two-Tank (Tank A: Calcium Nitrate + Chelated Fe-EDDHA; Tank B: Phosphates, Sulphates, Potash, MgSO4, Micronutrients + Acid) Venturi/Dosatron fertigation setup ([Fertigation Scheduling Guide](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/342162813_Fertigation_Scheduling_For_Horticultural_Crops)).
    * **Parthenocarpic Polyhouse Cucumber Fertigation ([Shriram Polyhouse Cucumber](https://shriramfarmsolutions.com/blog/drip-fertigation-in-polyhouse-cucumber-how-advanced-agricultural-technology-is-transforming-indian-farming/)):** Cultivate gynoecious parthenocarpic hybrids (no pollinator needed, $90–100\text{ day}$ crop cycle, $3\text{ cycles/year}$), pruning lateral shoots up to the 6th–8th node and applying $19:19:19$ up to $21\text{ DAT}$, $12:61:00 + 13:00:45$ at flowering ($22–40\text{ DAT}$), and $13:00:45 + 00:00:50 + \text{Calcium Nitrate}$ during continuous fruit harvest ($41–95\text{ DAT}$).

## 6. Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM), ETLs & Safe Chemical Calculator

### [CHUNK-11] Economic Threshold Levels (ETL) & IPM Decision Matrix for Major Indian Crops

  1. **Cultural & Mechanical Controls:** Deep summer ploughing (exposes pupae and nematodes to solar desiccation and predatory birds), timely synchronous sowing, clipping rice seedling leaf tips before transplanting (removes Yellow Stem Borer egg masses), installing **T-shaped bird perches @ $20–25\text{/ha}$ ($10\text{/acre}$)**, **Pheromone traps @ $5\text{ traps/ha}$ ($2\text{/acre}$)** for monitoring ($20\text{ traps/ha}$ for mass trapping, replacing lures every $15–20\text{ days}$), and **Yellow/Blue Sticky Traps @ $25–40\text{ traps/ha}$** for whiteflies, aphids, jassids, and thrips ([NCIPM Okra & Brinjal Folder](https://nriipm.res.in/NCIPMPDFs/Publication/okrabrinjalExtensionFolder_.pdf)).

     * **Egg Parasitoids (*Trichogramma japonicum* & *Trichogramma chilonis*):** Release $50,000–1,00,000\text{ parasitized eggs (Tricho-cards)/ha/week}$ starting $30\text{ DAT/DAS}$ for $4–6\text{ weeks}$ to control lepidopteran stem borers and bollworms across rice, sugarcane, cotton, maize, and vegetables ([TNAU IPM SOP](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/crop_protection/pdf/opproc_IPM.pdf)).

## 7. IMD Agromet Weather Contingency, Disaster Resilience & Perimeter Crop Protection

### [CHUNK-12] IMD Gramin Krishi Mausam Sewa (GKMS) Weather-Triggered Contingency Rules
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: agromet_weather_advisory` | `agency: imd_gkms_damu_amfu` | `events: cyclone_flood_drought_heatwave_frost_hail`
* **IMD Agromet Bulletin Architecture ([IMD GKMS Standard Operating Procedure](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/pdf/gkms_sop.pdf); [IMDAgrimet Portal](https://imdagrimet.gov.in/Files/SOP/SOP_GKMS_Final.pdf)):** Agrometeorological Field Units (AMFUs) and District Agro-Met Units (DAMUs) issue bi-weekly (Tuesday and Friday) 5-day Medium-Range Weather Forecast (MRWF) advisories at district and block levels across 8 parameters: Rainfall ($\text{mm}$), Maximum & Minimum Temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$), Morning & Evening Relative Humidity ($\%$), Wind Speed ($\text{km/h}$) & Direction, and Cloud Cover ($\text{octa}$).
* **Weather-to-Action Rules for Local Llama RAG Generation:**
  1. **Heavy Rainfall / Cyclone / Depression Alert ($\ge 65\text{ mm/day}$ or Wind $> 45\text{ km/h}$):**
     * **Pre-Event (48–72 hours prior):** Postpone all irrigation, fertilizer top-dressing, and pesticide spraying immediately ([IMD Agromet SOP](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/pdf/gkms_sop.pdf)). Harvest physiologically mature paddy ($>80\%$ yellow grains), jute, vegetables, and banana bunches immediately and shift to covered threshing floors. Clear peripheral field drainage channels, reinforce vegetable bowers/trellises and banana pseudostems with bamboo propping, and roll up or secure polyhouse side curtains properly to prevent wind uplift.
     * **Post-Event Recovery (Within 24–72 hours after storm/flood recession):** Drain stagnant water from vegetable, pulse, maize, potato, and nursery beds immediately. For lodged paddy at dough/ripening stage, tie $4–5\text{ hills}$ together in upright bundles to prevent grain sprouting on wet mud. Spray $2\%\text{ Urea}$ or $1\%\text{ NPK } 19:19:19$ on nutrient-leached regenerating crops, and apply prophylactic Copper Oxychloride $50\%\text{ WP @ } 2.5–3.0\text{ g/L}$ against post-cyclone Bacterial Leaf Blight and root/foot rot ([Post-Cyclone Rice Advisory](https://arccjournals.com/journal/bhartiya-krishi-anusandhan-patrika/BKAP827)).
  2. **Monsoon Delay / Mid-Season Dry Spell / Drought Contingency:**
     * **2-Week Monsoon Delay (Late June / Early July):** Switch from long-duration paddy ($145\text{ days}$) to short/medium drought-tolerant varieties (*Sahbhagi Dhan*, *Anjali*, *Shatabdi*) or direct-seeded drum-seeder rice.
     * **Mid-Season Dry Spell ($\ge 10–14\text{ rainless days}$ during Vegetative/Reproductive Stage):** Close soil cracks via shallow inter-cultivation (*dust mulching*), apply organic straw/leaf mulch @ $4–5\text{ t/ha}$ ([ICAR Crop Management](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Crop_Management.pdf)), provide life-saving micro-irrigation from farm ponds, and apply foliar spray of **$2\%\text{ KCl}$ (Muriate of Potash @ $20\text{ g/L}$)** or **$1\%\text{ KNO}_3$ ($10\text{ g/L}$)** or **Kaolin $3–5\%$ reflective antitranspirant** to regulate stomatal closure and maintain turgor.
  3. **Cold Wave / Ground Frost Alert (Night Temperature $< 4^\circ\text{C}$ in Dec–Jan) & Terminal Heat Wave ($> 35^\circ\text{C}$ in March–May):**
     * **Cold Wave / Frost Protection (Potato, Mustard, Tomato, Brinjal, Chickpea):** Apply light evening irrigation (moist soil holds higher specific heat capacity and radiates warmth overnight, raising canopy temperature by $1.5–2.5^\circ\text{C}$), cover low-tunnel nurseries with polypropylene non-woven row covers or polythene sheets at dusk, and spray $0.1\%\text{ Sulfuric Acid}$ ($1\text{ ml/L}$) or Thiourea @ $0.5\text{ g/L}$ to enhance cellular cryoprotection.
     * **Heat Wave Protection:** Irrigate in evening hours, maintain $5\text{ cm}$ ponded water in *Boro* paddy during flowering, erect $50\%$ green/white shade nets over nurseries and polyhouses, and spray $0.2\%\text{ Salicylic Acid}$ or $2\%\text{ KNO}_3$ at anthesis.

### [CHUNK-13] Stray Cattle, Feral Herd & Wildlife Perimeter Crop Protection Protocols
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: crop_protection_perimeter` | `threat: stray_cattle_zebu_crossbred_bulls_nilgai_boar` | `region: west_bengal_gangetic_plains`
* **Integrated Non-Lethal Bio-Acoustic, Olfactory & Living-Fence Advisory:** In intensive agricultural belts such as the Lower Gangetic Plains (Hooghly, Howrah, Burdwan, Nadia) and North/Central India, stray and abandoned cattle (including hardy indigenous *Deshi* Zebu, aggressive Hariana and Sahiwal crossbred bulls, and dry/abandoned Jersey and Holstein-Friesian crosses) as well as Nilgai and wild boar cause severe nocturnal trampling and grazing losses in *Aman*/*Boro* paddy, potato, mustard, and winter vegetable plots.
  1. **Multi-Tier Bio-Fencing (Living Perimeter Barrier):** Plant dense, thorny, unpalatable perimeter hedges along field bunds: **Karonda (*Carissa carandas*)**, **Inga dulcis (*Pithecellobium dulce* / Manila Tamarind)**, **Agave (*Agave americana* / *Sisal*)**, **Prickly Pear Cactus (*Opuntia*)**, or **Kewda (*Pandanus*)** at $30–45\text{ cm}$ staggered spacing, combined with border rows of **Castor (*Ricinus communis*)** or **Safflower (*Carthamus tinctorius* – spiny varieties)** which cattle avoid grazing.
  2. **Olfactory Bio-Repellent Sprays (Boundary Band Application):** Cattle rely heavily on olfactory cues before grazing. Spray a 3-meter peripheral crop strip every $10–12\text{ days}$ with **5% Neem Oil + 2% fermented cow dung/urine filtrate + crushed garlic-chilli extract**, or suspend porous jute/cloth sachets soaked in **Phenyl / Creosote / fermented castor cake emulsion** on bamboo stakes at $1\text{ m}$ height (cattle muzzle level) every $5\text{ meters}$ along the field boundary.
  3. **Solar Pulse Fencing & Edge-AI Camera Alerting:** Install BIS-compliant agricultural **Solar Battery-Powered High-Voltage Low-Current Pulse Energizer Fencing** ($0.5–1.5\text{ Joules}$, $1.2\text{ second}$ non-lethal pulse interval across 3–4 galvanized strands at $30, 60, 90,\text{ and }120\text{ cm}$ heights) with yellow warning signs, or pair an edge camera (Raspberry Pi 5 running quantized YOLO/NCNN cattle detection) with an automated ultrasonic/siren strobe relay and SMS/Telegram farmer alert.

## 8. Post-Harvest Storage Moisture Norms, Government Schemes & Helpline Directory

### [CHUNK-14] Safe Harvesting & Storage Moisture Thresholds & Hermetic Grain Protection
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: post_harvest_management` | `metrics: moisture_content_storage_pest_control`
* **Safe Grain Moisture Thresholds ([TNAU Crop Harvesting Criteria](http://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropharvesting.html)):** Storing grain above safe equilibrium moisture triggers *Aspergillus flavus* aflatoxin contamination and rapid multiplication of Rice Weevil (*Sitophilus oryzae*), Lesser Grain Borer (*Rhyzopertha dominica*), Angoumois Grain Moth (*Sitotroga cerealella*), and Pulse Beetle (*Callosobruchus chinensis*).
  * **Paddy (Raw Rice):** $14\%$ moisture content.
  * **Paddy (Parboiled Rice):** $15\%$ moisture content.
  * **Wheat, Barley, Maize, Sorghum, Pearl Millet, Finger Millet & Pulses:** $12\%$ moisture content.
  * **Groundnut Pods & Oilseeds (Mustard, Sesame, Soybean, Sunflower):** $8\% – 9\%$ moisture content (below $8\%$ prevents rancidity and mould).
  * **Coriander, Fenugreek & Spices:** $8\% – 10\%$ moisture content.
* **Safe Storage Protocols:** Use multi-layer Hermetic Storage Bags (*Pusa Bin*, *SuperGrainBag*, or triple-layer HDPE bags) which deplete internal $\text{O}_2$ below $5\%$ and suffocate bruchids and weevils without chemicals. For pulse seed storage, coat dried seeds ($<10\%$ moisture) with $5–10\text{ ml}$ edible mustard/groundnut/neem oil per $\text{kg}$ of seed or mix with $1\%$ activated kaolin clay to prevent *Callosobruchus* egg laying.

## 9. Fine-Tuning / RAG Evaluation QA Benchmark Pairs (Instruction-Tuning Gold Set)

### [CHUNK-16] Curated Instruction-Response Pairs for Local Llama Evaluation & Few-Shot Prompting
* **Metadata Tags:** `domain: llm_evaluation_qa` | `format: instruction_context_response` | `purpose: rag_benchmark_and_lora_finetuning`
* **Pair 1 — Soil Health Card & Commercial Fertilizer Calculation:**
  * **Farmer Query:** *"My Soil Health Card shows Available Nitrogen is 210 kg/ha, Phosphorus (P2O5) is 35 kg/ha, Potassium (K2O) is 190 kg/ha, and pH is 5.8. I am planting Kharif Aman paddy (Swarna) on 1 hectare. How should I adjust my fertilizer and how many kg of Urea, SSP, and MOP do I need?"*
  * **Grounded Gold Advisory Response:** According to [Soil Health Card Thresholds](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/Manual/140723DraftTeacherManual_PDF.pdf) and [Soil Fertility NIV Guidelines](https://www.biochemjournal.com/archives/2026/vol10issue10S/PartE/S-10-9-321-865.pdf), your soil is **Acidic** ($\text{pH } 5.8 < 6.5$), **Low in Nitrogen** ($210 < 280\text{ kg/ha}$), **Medium in Phosphorus** ($35\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5\text{/ha}$ falls within $23–57\text{ kg/ha}$), and **Medium in Potassium** ($190\text{ kg K}_2\text{O/ha}$ falls within $120–280\text{ kg/ha}$).
    1. **Soil pH Correction:** Apply $250–400\text{ kg/ha}$ of Agricultural Lime or Dolomite during land preparation $15\text{ days}$ before puddling so Phosphorus is not locked up ([Soil Health Card pH Guide](https://shop.ouranosrobotics.com/blogs/krishitalks/what-does-low-ideal-and-excess-mean-on-your-soil-health-card)).
    2. **Adjusted NPK Dose:** Starting from the standard *Aman* HYV recommendation of $80:40:40\text{ kg NPK/ha}$ ([ICAR Kharif Agro-Advisories 2025](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/ICAR-En-Kharif-Agro-Advisories-for-Farmers-2025.pdf)), increase Nitrogen by $+25\%$ for Low N status ($80 \times 1.25 = 100\text{ kg N/ha}$) and retain $100\%$ of P and K ($40\text{ kg P}_2\text{O}_5\text{/ha}$ and $40\text{ kg K}_2\text{O/ha}$).
    3. **Commercial Fertilizer Quantities for 1 Hectare:**
       * **Neem-Coated Urea ($46\%\text{ N}$):** $100 \times 2.174 = 217.4\text{ kg/ha}$ (apply $54.4\text{ kg}$ basal at puddling, $108.7\text{ kg}$ at active tillering $21\text{ DAT}$, and $54.3\text{ kg}$ at panicle initiation $42\text{ DAT}$).
       * **Single Super Phosphate – SSP ($16\%\text{ P}_2\text{O}_5$):** $40 \times 6.25 = 250\text{ kg/ha}$ (apply $100\%$ basal).
       * **Muriate of Potash – MOP ($60\%\text{ K}_2\text{O}$):** $40 \times 1.667 = 66.7\text{ kg/ha}$ (apply $33.4\text{ kg}$ basal and $33.3\text{ kg}$ at panicle initiation).
       * **Micronutrient Supplement:** Apply $\text{ZnSO}_4 \cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O} \text{ @ } 25\text{ kg/ha}$ mixed with dry sand (do not mix directly with SSP).
* **Pair 2 — Polyhouse IoT Sensor Anomaly & Disease Prevention:**
  * **Farmer Query:** *"My polyhouse IoT dashboard for Gerbera shows DHT22 Temperature = 34.2°C, Relative Humidity = 88%, Soil Moisture = 86%, and SEN0161 pH = 7.1. What immediate actions should I take?"*
  * **Grounded Gold Advisory Response:** Per [TNAU Gerbera Polyhouse Norms](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/horticulture/horti_flower%20crops_gerbera.html) and [Agrifirst Gerbera Microclimate Standards](https://agrifirst.in/blog/economics-of-gerbera-polyhouse-farming), three parameters are in critical stress zones:
    1. **Heat + High Humidity Stress ($34.2^\circ\text{C} > 25^\circ\text{C}$ target; $88\%\text{ RH} > 70\%$ target):** Open top and side roll-up vents fully and deploy the $50\%$ aluminet/white shade net immediately to reduce solar heat load, but **do not run overhead foggers** while RH is at $88\%$ (misting above $85\%\text{ RH}$ causes leaf wetness and triggers *Botrytis* blight and powdery mildew).
    2. **Root-Zone Waterlogging ($86\%\text{ VWC}$):** Suspend drip irrigation cycles for $24–36\text{ hours}$ until volumetric moisture drops to $60–65\%$. High soil moisture around buried Gerbera crowns triggers fatal *Phytophthora cryptogea* crown rot; verify crowns remain $1–2\text{ cm}$ above bed level and drench root collars prophylactically with *Trichoderma harzianum* @ $5\text{ g/L}$ or Copper Oxychloride $50\%\text{ WP @ } 2.5\text{ g/L}$.
    3. **Alkaline Fertigation pH ($\text{pH } 7.1 > 6.5$):** Inject food-grade Nitric or Phosphoric Acid into the fertigation tank ($0.2–0.4\text{ ml/L}$) to bring solution pH down to $5.8–6.2$ so Iron ($\text{Fe}$) and Boron ($\text{B}$) remain soluble ([ICAR Drip Fertigation Guide](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IndFarm/article/download/163803/62903/493520)).
* **Pair 3 — Post-Hailstorm / Unseasonal Rain Crop Loss Claim & Recovery:**
  * **Farmer Query:** *"Unseasonal hailstorm and heavy rain flattened my Rabi mustard and potato field in Hooghly yesterday. What should I do in the field and how do I claim insurance?"*
  * **Grounded Gold Advisory Response:**
    1. **Immediate Insurance Notification (Within 72 Hours):** Under [Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Operational Guidelines](https://pmfby.gov.in/pdf/Revised_Operational_Guidelines.pdf), hailstorm and inundation are covered under **Localized Calamities**. You must intimate the crop loss **within 72 hours** of the event via the Crop Insurance Mobile App, toll-free helpline `14447`, your KCC lending bank, or the Block ADA office (also verify state coverage under *Bangla Shasya Bima* / [Krishak Bandhu](https://www.bighaat.com/kisan-vedika/blogs/krishak-bandhu-scheme-in-west-bengal-benefits-and-how-to-apply)).
    2. **Immediate Field Recovery ([IMD Agromet SOP](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/pdf/gkms_sop.pdf); [Vikaspedia Potato Guide](https://agriculture.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/agriculture/crop-production/package-of-practices/vegetables-1/potato?lgn=en)):** Open furrow ends to drain all standing water within $12–24\text{ hours}$ so potato tubers do not suffocate or rot. Once foliage dries, spray Cymoxanil $8\% + \text{Mancozeb } 64\%\text{ WP @ } 2.5\text{ g/L}$ on potato to prevent Late Blight outbreak under cool humid conditions, and spray Carbendazim $12\% + \text{Mancozeb } 63\%\text{ WP @ } 2.0\text{ g/L}$ + $1\%\text{ NPK } 19:19:19$ on damaged mustard plants to prevent *Alternaria* blight and aid secondary branching. For live local extension guidance, call the [Kisan Call Centre at `1800-180-1551`](https://www.manage.gov.in/kcc/kcc.asp).
